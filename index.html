What are you after?
Power-breathing techniques (Wim Hof, Tummo, Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Breath of Fire) are done seated or lying down, on land, away from water — never in a bath, pool, lake, sauna, or while driving or standing. Low CO₂ removes the urge to breathe, so blackout can arrive without warning. Skip breath holds and forceful breathing if you are pregnant, have heart disease, arrhythmia, uncontrolled high blood pressure, epilepsy, or a history of fainting. Never cold-plunge alone in open water.
